Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as your email address during registration and the financial transactions you log in the application (amounts, categories, and dates). We also collect basic technical data like your IP address and browser type to ensure the app works correctly.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is used exclusively to provide the Spendly service to you. This includes managing your account, calculating your spending totals, and generating spending reports. We do not use your personal data for marketing or sell it to third-party advertisers.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in our database. We implement industry-standard encryption and security protocols to prevent unauthorized access to your financial information.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not share your personal data with third parties except where required by law or to provide critical infrastructure services (such as database hosting) that are bound by strict confidentiality agreements.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to request the deletion of your data, please contact us at support@spendly.example.com.
